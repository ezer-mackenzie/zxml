# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

As participants and maintainers, we are committed to making participation in
this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance,
orientation, disability, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and in good faith.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus on ideas, not people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and considerately.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to people who are new to the
  project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, sharing private
information without consent, and any other conduct that reasonably makes others
feel unsafe participating are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and take appropriate action in
response to conduct that violates this code, including restricting
participation in the project. Responses should be proportionate to the
situation.

To report an incident, contact the maintainer privately through a private
channel available in the repository. Do not post personal details or sensitive
information in a public issue. Reports will be handled discreetly to the extent
possible.
